BEFORE YOU REACH OUT

A few useful answers.

Can you quote from a few photos?

Photos and a description can help with the initial discussion. A reliable scope may require an assessment of the source, materials and access conditions.

Do you work with restoration and cleaning companies?

Yes, partner inquiries are welcome. Tell us your role, the project stage and the part of the odor concern you need help with.

Is complete odor removal guaranteed?

No blanket guarantee is offered. Hidden sources, moisture or affected materials can require additional work. We discuss limitations as part of the scope.

What if there is a moisture or mold concern?

Tell us before scheduling. An odor assessment is not a mold inspection, health evaluation or indoor-air certification. Related moisture or mold concerns may require an appropriately qualified specialist.
