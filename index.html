Terms of Service & Estimate Architecture Agreement

Effective Date: June 2026

1. Professional Scope of Services

TriVault Property Solutions LLC provides line-item auditing, independent property estimating, and technological data architecture using Xactimate® platform software. All data entries, sketches, and cost models are synthesized exclusively from source materials—including structural dimensions, field photography, moisture logs, or material reports—provided directly by the Client.

2. Payment Terms, Triage Deposits & Revisions

  • Non-Refundable Intake Deposit: A minimum payment of $200 is processed at checkout to secure file triage, engineering allocation, and initial data verification. This deposit is non-refundable.
  • Final Invoicing Parameters: The final billing balance is dynamically adjusted to exactly 2% of the gross total value calculated in the finalized Xactimate® document.
  • Review Window & Automatic Finalization: Upon electronic delivery of the preliminary draft, the Client has exactly seven (7) calendar days to request corrections or line-item edits. If no written modification directives are logged within this 7-day window, the file is automatically flagged as Approved as Written, and the remaining balance will be immediately processed using the card on file.

3. Third-Party Accuracy & Indemnification

TriVault acts strictly as an objective technical data provider. The Client bears sole legal and operational responsibility for verifying that all field data, loss details, and measurements provided to TriVault are factual, accurate, and fully honest.

4. Regulatory Disclosure & Non-Representation

The Client explicitly acknowledges that compiling technical Xactimate® documents under this agreement does not constitute public adjusting services, statutory legal counsel, or public claim representation.
